Our story

From a California road trip to a burger stand in Jeddah.

How CALIVVS started

CALIVVS began with four friends, a road trip along the California coast and one unforgettable roadside burger. Back home in Jeddah we could not find anything like it — so we decided to make it ourselves.

The name blends Cali with the double V of our signature Double Wave burger. Our idea is simple: a short menu, fresh ingredients and fast, friendly service.

“Do a few things, and do them really well.”

— The CALIVVS founders

What we stand for

  • Fresh beef delivered every day — never frozen
  • Potatoes cut by hand in our kitchen every morning
  • Shakes made from real ice cream
  • Buns baked fresh and toasted to order
  • An open kitchen you can see from the counter

How we make your burger

  1. Press. A fresh beef patty is pressed and seasoned.
  2. Grill. It is cooked on the flat-top grill and the cheese is melted on top.
  3. Build. A toasted bun, CALIVVS spread, lettuce, tomato and onion.
  4. Serve. Your burger is wrapped and in your hands in about 7 minutes.
