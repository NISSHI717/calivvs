Frequently asked questions

Is your beef halal?

Yes. All of our beef is 100% halal certified and delivered fresh every day.

Any student with a valid university or college ID, Sunday to Wednesday, for dine-in and pickup orders.

How early should I book catering?

Please send your request at least 48 hours before the event so we can prepare everything fresh.

Can I combine two offers?
